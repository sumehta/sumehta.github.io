Projects / Code

Simplify-then-Translate

Automatic sentence simplification as preprocessing for black-box MT. Up to +4 BLEU on subtitles across 7 low-resource languages.

TweetNERD

An end-to-end entity linking benchmark for tweets, released as a NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks paper.

Event detection with hierarchical multi-aspect attention

Detecting and extracting socio-political events from news articles.

Machine-generated analytic reports

Writes a two-page answer to an analyst's question from a large collection of articles. Finalist in the challenge.

Civil War Photo Sleuth

Combines face recognition with crowdsourced expertise to identify historical portraits. About 15,000 users. Best Paper, ACM IUI 2019.

Siamese network for binary VQA

A Siamese architecture for yes/no visual question answering, trained in Torch (68.1% accuracy).

Human performance in image geolocation

An exploratory study of how people geolocate images, to guide crowd + AI tool design.

Birds in a Forest

Bird species identification from images with computer vision and machine learning.
